What We Do

Professional & Social Connections

Maintain and increase professional and social contacts among retired teachers.

Stay Informed

Keep informed of developments in education and matters affecting retired educators.

Government Advocacy

Meet with Government and negotiate better terms for retired teachers.

Member Benefits

Meet with outside organizations and merchants to obtain social and financial benefits for members.

PEITF Liaison

Maintain a liaison with the PEI Teachers' Federation.

Policy Advisory

Advise the provincial executive and PEITF committees on matters affecting retired teachers' welfare.
